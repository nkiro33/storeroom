Why sellers are backing up now

TPT's new seller terms start on January 1, 2027. Sellers who opt in to the new Exclusive Seller membership (77.5% payout) may sell their teaching resources only on TPT; everyone else becomes a Basic seller (55% payout). Undecided?

Back up first; decide later. Storeroom reads your store at a gentle pace and keeps everything in your browser until you export it.

Private by design

  • No passwords. Storeroom works inside your own browser while you're signed in to TPT. It never sees or stores your password.
  • No servers. Your backup stays in your browser until you export it to your own Downloads folder. No analytics, no tracking.
  • Only your store. It backs up a store only when TPT's own page data says you are its author.
  • Gentle. One page about every 3 seconds, like a person browsing, and it pauses itself if TPT is busy.
  • Never publishes. It never edits, deletes or publishes anything on TPT or any other site.

Questions

Is this allowed by TPT?

Storeroom reads the same public store and product pages you can open yourself, only for the store you're signed in to, at a person's pace. TPT's Terms of Service say sellers keep the rights to their own content. Storeroom doesn't copy TPT's own site design or code, and it never publishes anything for you. It is not affiliated with TPT.

Do I need to give Storeroom my TPT password?

No. Never. Storeroom works in your browser where you're already signed in. Be careful with any tool that asks for your TPT password.

How long does a backup take?

About 3 seconds per page: 1,000 products take a little under an hour. You can keep working in other tabs, pause any time, and it picks up where it stopped.

Does it save my product files (the PDFs buyers download)?

Storeroom saves your listings, images and previews. Pro can also try to save your product files through TPT's download links while you're signed in. That part is in beta, because TPT controls those links. Your original files on your own computer are the safest copy.

Will my Shopify or WooCommerce products be ready to sell?

Titles, descriptions, prices, images and tags come across, as drafts by default so you can review them. You attach the actual product files in your new shop (the guides show how).

What if I'm going Exclusive?

Keep a backup anyway. TPT's Exclusive rules still allow your own website if its Buy buttons send people to TPT, and Storeroom's WooCommerce "Buy on TPT" catalog does exactly that.

Which browsers?

Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on Windows, Mac and Chromebook.
