List your TPT products on Etsy, Payhip, Gumroad and more

Updated October 15, 2026 · 6-minute read

Many sellers are adding a second place to sell. The catch: Etsy, Payhip, Gumroad, TES, Eduki and Classful have no product import, so every listing is a form to fill in. Storeroom makes that fast in two ways.

Way 1: copy sheets (free)

In Storeroom › Export, the "Etsy, Payhip, Gumroad, TES, Eduki, Classful" card makes a spreadsheet for the site you pick, with each field already shaped for it:

SitePrepared for you
EtsyTitle cut to Etsy's 140 characters; up to 13 tags of 20 characters or fewer; plain-text description; price; image links
PayhipTitle, formatted description, price, images
GumroadName, formatted description, price, a short summary, a URL slug and tags
TESTitle, description, student age range (from your grades), main subject, resource type, tags
EdukiTitle, description, grades, subject, tags
ClassfulTitle, description, grades, subject, resource type, tags

Open the sheet in Excel or Google Sheets and copy each cell into the site's new-listing form.

Way 2: the crosslist assistant (Pro, beta)

  1. Sign in to the site and open its new-listing page.
  2. Open Storeroom › Crosslist › Open the crosslist assistant. It opens beside the page.
  3. Pick a product and press Fill this page. The first time, Chrome asks permission for that one site.
  4. The assistant fills the title, description, price and tags, and shows which fields it filled. Fields it couldn't find have Copy buttons.
  5. Upload your product file and images (the assistant can download the images for you), check everything, and press the site's own Publish button. Storeroom never publishes for you.
  6. Tick Listed on… to track your progress across the store.
Beta: these sites change their forms without warning. If a field doesn't fill, use its Copy button and tell us so we can fix it.

Before you list

  • Exclusive Sellers: TPT's Exclusive membership doesn't allow selling your digital teaching resources elsewhere. Clip art, fonts, Boom Cards, courses, physical products and Kindle books are exempt. Details.
  • Each marketplace has its own fees and rules; check them before you list. Etsy, for example, charges a small fee per listing.
  • Watch for generative-AI rules: TPT asked sellers in October 2026 not to use AI-generated images or text in covers, thumbnails, previews or descriptions. Other sites have their own policies.
