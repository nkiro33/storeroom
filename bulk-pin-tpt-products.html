Bulk-pin your TPT products to Pinterest

Updated October 15, 2026 · 5-minute read

Pinterest can create up to 200 pins at once from a spreadsheet (a .csv file). All it needs is a title, an image link, a board and a link for each pin. Storeroom builds that file from your TPT store, with your cover images already linked.

1. Back up your store

Follow How to back up your TPT store.

2. Make the Pinterest file

  1. Open Storeroom › Export › Pinterest bulk pins and open Options.
  2. Boards: one per subject ("Math Resources"), a subject board with a section per grade, one per grade, or one board you name. Pinterest creates any board or section that doesn't exist yet.
  3. Pins link to: your TPT product page, or your new shop (for example https://yourshop.com/products/{handle}; {handle} matches Storeroom's Shopify export).
  4. Pins per product: just the cover, or every thumbnail (up to 4).
  5. When: publish right away, or schedule a few per day from a start date.
  6. Press Download Pinterest CSV. More than 200 pins are split into several files, because Pinterest accepts up to 200 per upload.

What's in each row

Pinterest columnFrom your store
Title (100 characters max)Product title
Media URLYour full-size cover image
Pinterest boardYour board choice, e.g. Math/3rd–5th grade
Description (500 characters max)The start of your description plus the grade range
LinkTPT or your new shop, with optional UTM tags so you can see Pinterest visits in your stats
Publish dateEmpty (now) or your schedule
KeywordsSubjects, grade range, resource types, seasons

3. Upload to Pinterest

  1. Use a Pinterest business account (switching is free) on a computer; bulk upload isn't available on mobile.
  2. Open Settings › Import content and click Upload next to "Upload .csv or .txt file".
  3. Choose the Storeroom file. Upload the next file once the first has finished.
Pinterest's own help page lists these columns and limits (checked October 15, 2026). If Pinterest changes them, Storeroom will update.
