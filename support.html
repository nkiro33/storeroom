Storeroom help

Quick answers first, then how to reach us.

Backups

I don't see the Storeroom card on my store page

Make sure you're signed in to TPT in the same browser, then open your public store page (teacherspayteachers.com/store/your-store-name) and reload it. The card only appears on a store TPT says you own. You can also click the Storeroom icon in the toolbar and press "Back up this store".

It says the store belongs to someone else

Storeroom only backs up the store you're signed in to. If it's your store, sign out of TPT and back in, then try again. If it still happens, tell us (below).

Can I use my computer during a backup?

Yes. Keep the TPT tab where it's running open; use other tabs and apps as usual. If you close it, open any TPT page later and Storeroom continues where it stopped.

It paused and says TPT is busy

Storeroom waits and retries when TPT asks it to slow down, then pauses after a few tries. Press Resume later. Nothing is lost.

Some product pages "could not be read"

Usually products that were removed or hidden. Their listing details (title, price, short description, images) are still in your backup.

Exports

Where are my files?

In your Downloads folder, inside Storeroom › your-store-name.

Shopify or WooCommerce says something is wrong with the file

Send us the error message and which export you used. Don't edit the column names; both importers rely on them.

Chrome asks where to save every file

Turn off "Ask where to save each file before downloading" in Chrome Settings › Downloads while you export, especially before saving product files.

Free and Pro

What does the free plan include?

Storeroom finds every product in your store and gives you full details, the backup spreadsheet and every export format for 25 products, so you can try it all with your own store.

How do I activate Pro?

Buy Pro on Gumroad; your license key arrives by email right after checkout. Open Storeroom › Account (or the Upgrade page), paste the key and press Activate. Then press "Back up the other…" on the Backup tab to finish your full backup. One key works on up to 5 browsers.

Refunds

30 days, no questions: reply to your Gumroad receipt.

Report a problem or ask a question

  • Pro customers: reply to your Gumroad receipt email; it reaches us directly.
  • Everyone: open an issue at github.com/nkiro33/storeroom/issues (a free GitHub account is needed).

Please include your store page link, what you pressed, and what you saw (a screenshot helps). Never send your TPT password; we will never ask for it.
