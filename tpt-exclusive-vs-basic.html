TPT Exclusive vs Basic: what changes on January 1, 2027

Updated October 15, 2026 · Sources at the bottom. Always check TPT's own pages before deciding.

On October 1, 2026, TPT announced that its Premium seller membership will be replaced by a new Exclusive Seller membership. Here's what TPT has said so far, in one place.

The key dates

DateWhat happens
October 1, 2026Exclusive Seller announced. Premium closed to new sign-ups.
Through December 31, 2026Current Premium sellers keep their 80% payout.
December 31, 2026, 11:59pm ETLast moment for current Premium sellers to opt in to Exclusive before the switch.
January 1, 2027Premium ends. Sellers who didn't opt in become Basic sellers.

Exclusive vs Basic at a glance

Exclusive SellerBasic Seller
Payout rate77.5%55%
Membership fee$59.95 per year$29 one-time (waived for Premium sellers who are converted, per the town-hall recap)
Transaction feeNone on orders over $3; $0.15 per resource under $3$0.30 per resource
Selling elsewhereTPT-eligible digital teaching resources only on TPTAllowed

Example from one seller blog: on a $10 sale, an Exclusive seller keeps $7.75; a Basic seller keeps $5.20 after the $0.30 fee.

What "exclusive" covers

  • Can't be sold off TPT: digital teaching resources you sell on TPT, memberships that include them, and licenses for them.
  • Still fine elsewhere: clip art and fonts, physical products, courses and professional development, Boom Cards, and Kindle books.
  • Your own website: may stay up. Its "Buy" buttons can send people to the matching product on TPT.
  • Free resources: allowed on and off TPT, but you can't charge on TPT for something you give away free elsewhere.
  • Leaving Exclusive after January 1: allowed any time, but then there's a one-year wait before you can rejoin.

The AI announcement

At an October 8 town hall, TPT asked sellers not to use generative AI for images or text in resource files, covers, thumbnails, previews or product descriptions, and to replace AI content already published. A written policy was still to come at the time of writing.

What to do before December 31

  1. Back up your store, whatever you decide: listings, images, previews and your product files. How.
  2. Run your numbers: what share of your income comes from outside TPT? Exclusive needs that to stop (except the exempt categories).
  3. If you go Exclusive: point your website's buttons to TPT. Storeroom's WooCommerce "Buy on TPT" catalog builds that for you.
  4. If you go Basic or leave: set up your second channel early: Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy, Payhip and others, and Pinterest for traffic.
